## 题目
计算极限 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x^3)}{\sin^2 x}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**
当 $x \to 0$ 时，$\sin(x^3) \sim x^3$，$\sin^2 x \sim x^2$：
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x^3)}{\sin^2 x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x^3}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} x = 0$$
