## 题目
计算极限 $\lim_{n \to \infty} [\sqrt{1+2+\cdots+n} - \sqrt{1+2+\cdots+(n-1)}]$.

## 答案与解析
**解**
利用等差数列求和化简被开方数：
$$\sqrt{1+2+\cdots+n} - \sqrt{1+2+\cdots+(n-1)} = \sqrt{\frac{n(n+1)}{2}} - \sqrt{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{n}{2}}(\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n-1})$$
分子有理化：
$$= \sqrt{\frac{n}{2}} \frac{(n+1)-(n-1)}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n-1}} = \sqrt{\frac{n}{2}} \frac{2}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n-1}} = \frac{\sqrt{2n}}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n-1}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}} + \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{n}}}$$
取极限得：
$$\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}} + \sqrt{1-\frac{1}{n}}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{1+1} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$
