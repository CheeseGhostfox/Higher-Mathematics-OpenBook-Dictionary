## 题目
求极限 $\lim_{x \to 1} (1-x)\tan\frac{\pi x}{2}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**
令 $t = 1-x \to 0$，则 $x = 1-t$：
$$\tan\frac{\pi x}{2} = \tan\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi t}{2}\right) = \cot\frac{\pi t}{2} = \frac{\cos\frac{\pi t}{2}}{\sin\frac{\pi t}{2}}$$
代入极限中：
$$\lim_{x \to 1} (1-x)\tan\frac{\pi x}{2} = \lim_{t \to 0} t \cdot \frac{\cos\frac{\pi t}{2}}{\sin\frac{\pi t}{2}} = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{t}{\sin\frac{\pi t}{2}} \cdot \lim_{t \to 0} \cos\frac{\pi t}{2} = \frac{1}{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cdot 1 = \frac{2}{\pi}$$
