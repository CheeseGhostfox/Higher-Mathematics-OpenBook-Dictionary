## 题目
求极限 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin \frac{x}{2}}{x}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**
凑项：
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin \frac{x}{2}}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin \frac{x}{2}}{2 \cdot \frac{x}{2}} = \frac{1}{2} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin \frac{x}{2}}{\frac{x}{2}} = \frac{1}{2} \times 1 = \frac{1}{2}$$
