## 题目
计算极限 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{\sqrt{2+x}-\sqrt{2-x}}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**
分母有理化：
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x(\sqrt{2+x}+\sqrt{2-x})}{(2+x)-(2-x)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x(\sqrt{2+x}+\sqrt{2-x})}{2x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sqrt{2+x}+\sqrt{2-x}}{2} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{2} = \sqrt{2}$$
