## 题目
求椭圆 $\begin{cases} x = a\cos t, \\ y = b\sin t \end{cases}$ 在点 $(0, b)$ 处的曲率及曲率半径.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
点 $(0, b)$ 对应参数 $t = \frac{\pi}{2}$。计算各阶导数在 $t = \frac{\pi}{2}$ 处的值：
$$\varphi'(t) = -a\sin t, \quad \varphi''(t) = -a\cos t \implies \varphi'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = -a, \quad \varphi''\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 0$$
$$\psi'(t) = b\cos t, \quad \psi''(t) = -b\sin t \implies \psi'\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = 0, \quad \psi''\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = -b$$
代入曲率公式得：
$$\left. K \right|_{t=\frac{\pi}{2}} = \frac{|\varphi'\psi'' - \varphi''\psi'|}{(\varphi'^2 + \psi'^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}} = \frac{ab}{a^3} = \frac{b}{a^2}$$
曲率半径为 $\rho = \frac{1}{K} = \frac{a^2}{b}$。
