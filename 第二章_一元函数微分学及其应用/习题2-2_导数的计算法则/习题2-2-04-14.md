## 题目
求复合函数 $y = \sqrt{\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}}$ 的导数.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\begin{aligned}
y' &= \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}} \cdot \left(\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}\right)' \\
&= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}}} \cdot \frac{\cos x(1-\sin x) - (1+\sin x)(-\cos x)}{(1-\sin x)^2} \\
&= \frac{\cos x}{\sqrt{1-\sin^2 x}(1-\sin x)} = \frac{\cos x}{|\cos x|(1-\sin x)} = \pm\frac{1}{1-\sin x}
\end{aligned}$$
