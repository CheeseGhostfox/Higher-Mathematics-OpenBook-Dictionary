## 题目
设 $y = \ln(1+2x-3x^2)$，求 $y^{(4)}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
因式分解得 $y = \ln[(1+3x)(1-x)] = \ln(1+3x) + \ln(1-x)$。逐阶求导：
$$y' = \frac{3}{1+3x} - \frac{1}{1-x}$$
$$y'' = -\frac{3^2}{(1+3x)^2} - \frac{1}{(1-x)^2}$$
$$y''' = \frac{2 \cdot 3^3}{(1+3x)^3} - \frac{2}{(1-x)^3}$$
$$y^{(4)} = -\frac{6 \cdot 3^4}{(1+3x)^4} - \frac{6}{(1-x)^4} = -6\left[\left(\frac{3}{3x+1}\right)^4 + \frac{1}{(x-1)^4}\right]$$
