## 题目
设 $y = e^x \cos x$，求 $y'''$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$y' = e^x(\cos x - \sin x)$$
$$y'' = e^x(\cos x - \sin x) + e^x(-\sin x - \cos x) = -2e^x\sin x$$
$$y''' = -2e^x(\sin x + \cos x)$$
