## 题目
设 $y = \sin x - \cos x$，求 $\left. y' \right|_{x=\frac{\pi}{6}}$，$\left. y' \right|_{x=\frac{\pi}{4}}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
由题意知 $y' = \cos x + \sin x$，则：
$$\left. y' \right|_{x=\frac{\pi}{6}} = \cos\frac{\pi}{6} + \sin\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2}$$
$$\left. y' \right|_{x=\frac{\pi}{4}} = \cos\frac{\pi}{4} + \sin\frac{\pi}{4} = \sqrt{2}$$
