## 题目
已知 $f(0) = 0$ 且 $f'(0) = 2$，求 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x}$ 和 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{\sin 3x}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
利用导数的定义及等价无穷小代换可知：
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} = f'(0) = 2$$
$$\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{\sin 3x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{3x} = \frac{1}{3} \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} = \frac{1}{3}f'(0) = \frac{2}{3}$$
