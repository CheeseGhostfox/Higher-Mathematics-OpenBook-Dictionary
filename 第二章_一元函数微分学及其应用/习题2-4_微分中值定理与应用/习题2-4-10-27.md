## 题目
利用洛必达法则求极限：$\lim_{x \to 0} \left(\frac{\sin x}{x}\right)^{\frac{1}{1-\cos x}}$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x \to 0} \left(\frac{\sin x}{x}\right)^{\frac{1}{1-\cos x}} &= \exp\left\{\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln\sin x - \ln x}{\frac{1}{2}x^2}\right\} = \exp\left\{\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\cot x - \frac{1}{x}}{x}\right\} \\
&= \exp\left\{\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x\cos x - \sin x}{x^3}\right\} = \exp\left\{\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{-x\sin x}{3x^2}\right\} = e^{-\frac{1}{3}}
\end{aligned}$$
