# 习题 2-4 第 10(23) 题

## 题目
利用洛必达法则求极限：$\lim_{x \to 0} \left(\frac{1}{x^2} - \cot^2 x\right)$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x \to 0} \left(\frac{1}{x^2} - \cot^2 x\right) &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin^2 x - x^2\cos^2 x}{x^2\sin^2 x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin^2 x - x^2\cos^2 x}{x^4} \\
&= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin 2x - 2x\cos^2 x + x^2\sin 2x}{4x^3} = \frac{1}{2} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin 2x - 2x\cos^2 x}{4x^3} \\
&= \frac{1}{2} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2\cos 2x - 2\cos^2 x + 2x\sin 2x}{12x^2} \\
&= \frac{5}{6} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2\cos 2x - 2\cos^2 x}{12x^2} = \frac{5}{6} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{-4\sin 2x + 2\sin 2x}{24x} = \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{3}
\end{aligned}$$
