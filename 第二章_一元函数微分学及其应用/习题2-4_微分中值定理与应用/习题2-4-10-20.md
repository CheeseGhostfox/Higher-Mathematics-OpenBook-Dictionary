## 题目
利用洛必达法则求极限：$\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left(\frac{2}{\pi}\arctan x\right)^x$.

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x \to +\infty} \left(\frac{2}{\pi}\arctan x\right)^x &= \exp\left\{\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\ln\frac{2}{\pi} + \ln\arctan x}{\frac{1}{x}}\right\} \\
&= \exp\left\{\lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\frac{1}{\arctan x} \cdot \frac{1}{1+x^2}}{-\frac{1}{x^2}}\right\} = e^{-\frac{2}{\pi}}
\end{aligned}$$
