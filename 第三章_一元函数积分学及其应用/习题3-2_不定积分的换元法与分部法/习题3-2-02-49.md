## 题目
用换元法求不定积分 $\int \frac{x^2}{\sqrt[3]{2-x}}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：令 $t = \sqrt[3]{2-x}$，则 $x = 2 - t^3$，$dx = -3t^2 dt$，于是：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{x^2}{\sqrt[3]{2-x}}dx &= \int \frac{(2-t^3)^2}{t}(-3t^2 dt) = \int (-3t^7 + 12t^4 - 12t)dt \\
&= -\frac{3}{8}t^8 + \frac{12}{5}t^5 - 6t^2 + C \\
&= -\frac{3}{8}(\sqrt[3]{2-x})^8 + \frac{12}{5}(\sqrt[3]{2-x})^5 - 6(\sqrt[3]{2-x})^2 + C
\end{aligned}$$
