## 题目
用换元法计算不定积分 $\int \frac{\sqrt{x+2}}{1+\sqrt{x+2}}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：令 $t = \sqrt{x+2}$，则 $x = t^2 - 2$，$dx = 2tdt$，从而：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{\sqrt{x+2}}{1+\sqrt{x+2}}dx &= 2\int \frac{t^2}{1+t}dt = 2\int \left(t - 1 + \frac{1}{1+t}\right)dt \\
&= t^2 - 2t + 2\ln(1+t) + C' \\
&= x - 2\sqrt{x+2} + 2\ln\left(1+\sqrt{x+2}\right) + C
\end{aligned}$$
