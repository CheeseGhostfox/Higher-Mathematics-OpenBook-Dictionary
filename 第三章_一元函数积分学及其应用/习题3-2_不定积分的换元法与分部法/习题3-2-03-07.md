## 题目
用换元法计算不定积分 $\int \frac{x^2-1}{x^4+1}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：分子分母同除以 $x^2$，得：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{x^2-1}{x^4+1}dx &= \int \frac{1-\frac{1}{x^2}}{x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}}dx = \int \frac{d\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)}{\left(x+\frac{1}{x}\right)^2 - 2} \\
&= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\frac{x+\frac{1}{x}-\sqrt{2}}{x+\frac{1}{x}+\sqrt{2}}\right| + C = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\ln\frac{x^2-\sqrt{2}x+1}{x^2+\sqrt{2}x+1} + C
\end{aligned}$$
