## 题目
用换元法求不定积分 $\int \frac{1}{1-\sqrt{2x+1}}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：令 $t = \sqrt{2x+1}$，则 $x = \frac{t^2-1}{2}$，$dx = tdt$，于是：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{1}{1-\sqrt{2x+1}}dx &= \int \frac{t}{1-t}dt = \int \left(-1 + \frac{1}{1-t}\right)dt \\
&= -t - \ln|1-t| + C = -\sqrt{2x+1} - \ln\left|1-\sqrt{2x+1}\right| + C
\end{aligned}$$
