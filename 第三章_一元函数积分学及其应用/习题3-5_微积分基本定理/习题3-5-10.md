## 题目
设 $f(x) = \begin{cases} \sin x, & 0 \le x < \frac{\pi}{2}, \\ x, & \frac{\pi}{2} \le x \le \pi, \end{cases}$ 求 $\int_0^\pi f(x)dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：利用定积分对区间的可加性：
$$\int_0^\pi f(x)dx = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin x dx + \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^\pi x dx = \left[-\cos x\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} + \left[\frac{1}{2}x^2\right]_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^\pi = 1 + \frac{3}{8}\pi^2$$
