## 题目
求极限 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\left(\int_0^x e^{t^2}dt\right)^2}{\int_0^x te^{2t^2}dt}$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：连续使用洛必达法则：
$$\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\left(\int_0^x e^{t^2}dt\right)^2}{\int_0^x te^{2t^2}dt} &= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2\left(\int_0^x e^{t^2}dt\right)e^{x^2}}{xe^{2x^2}} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2\int_0^x e^{t^2}dt}{x} \\
&= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2e^{x^2}}{1} = 2
\end{aligned}$$
