## 题目
用定积分的分部积分法求积分 $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{6}} \arctan 2x dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{6}} \arctan 2x dx &= \left[x\arctan 2x\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{6}} - \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{6}} \frac{2x}{1+4x^2}dx \\
&= \frac{\pi}{6}\arctan\frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\left[\ln(1+4x^2)\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{6}} \\
&= \frac{\pi}{6}\arctan\frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\ln\left(1 + \frac{\pi^2}{9}\right)
\end{aligned}$$
