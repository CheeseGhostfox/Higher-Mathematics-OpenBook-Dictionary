## 题目
已知 $\int_x^{2\ln 2} \frac{dt}{\sqrt{e^t-1}} = \frac{\pi}{6}$，求 $x$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：令 $\sqrt{e^t-1} = u$，则 $t = \ln(1+u^2)$，$dt = \frac{2udu}{1+u^2}$。当 $t = 2\ln 2$ 时 $u = \sqrt{3}$，从而：
$$\int_x^{2\ln 2} \frac{dt}{\sqrt{e^t-1}} = \int_{\sqrt{e^x-1}}^{\sqrt{3}} \frac{2du}{1+u^2} = \frac{2\pi}{3} - 2\arctan\sqrt{e^x-1} = \frac{\pi}{6}$$
解得 $\arctan\sqrt{e^x-1} = \frac{\pi}{4} \implies \sqrt{e^x-1} = 1 \implies x = \ln 2$。
