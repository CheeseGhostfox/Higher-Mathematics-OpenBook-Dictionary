## 题目
用定积分的分部积分法求积分 $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{8}} x\sin x \cdot \cos x \cdot \cos 2x dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：由 $\sin x\cos x\cos 2x = \frac{1}{4}\sin 4x$，得：
$$\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{8}} x\sin x\cos x\cos 2x dx = \frac{1}{4}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{8}} x\sin 4x dx = \left[-\frac{x}{16}\cos 4x\right]_0^{\frac{\pi}{8}} + \frac{1}{16}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{8}} \cos 4x dx = \frac{1}{64}$$
