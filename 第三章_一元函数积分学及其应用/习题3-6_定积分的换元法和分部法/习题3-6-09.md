## 题目
设 $f(x) = \begin{cases} xe^{-x^2}, & x \ge 0, \\ \dfrac{1}{1+\cos x}, & -1 < x < 0, \end{cases}$ 求 $\int_1^4 f(x-2)dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：令 $t = x - 2$，则：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int_1^4 f(x-2)dx &= \int_{-1}^2 f(t)dt = \int_{-1}^0 \frac{1}{1+\cos t}dt + \int_0^2 te^{-t^2}dt \\
&= \left[\tan\frac{t}{2}\right]_{-1}^0 + \left[-\frac{1}{2}e^{-t^2}\right]_0^2 = \tan\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{2}e^{-4}
\end{aligned}$$
