## 题目
计算定积分 $\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}} \sqrt{\ln^2(1-x)}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}} \sqrt{\ln^2(1-x)}dx &= \int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{1}{2}} |\ln(1-x)|dx = \int_{-\frac{1}{2}}^0 \ln(1-x)dx - \int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} \ln(1-x)dx \\
&= \left[(x-1)\ln(1-x) - x\right]_{-\frac{1}{2}}^0 - \left[(x-1)\ln(1-x) - x\right]_0^{\frac{1}{2}} \\
&= \frac{3}{2}\ln 3 - 2\ln 2
\end{aligned}$$
