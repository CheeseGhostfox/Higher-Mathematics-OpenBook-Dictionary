## 题目
用定积分的换元法计算积分 $\int_{\frac{1}{\pi}}^{\frac{2}{\pi}} \frac{1}{x^2}\sin\frac{1}{x}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：
$$\int_{\frac{1}{\pi}}^{\frac{2}{\pi}} \frac{1}{x^2}\sin\frac{1}{x}dx = -\int_{\frac{1}{\pi}}^{\frac{2}{\pi}} \sin\frac{1}{x}d\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \left[\cos\frac{1}{x}\right]_{\frac{1}{\pi}}^{\frac{2}{\pi}} = 0 - (-1) = 1$$
