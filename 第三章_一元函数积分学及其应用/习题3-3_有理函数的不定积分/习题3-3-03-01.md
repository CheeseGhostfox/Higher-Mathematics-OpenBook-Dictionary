## 题目
求无理函数的不定积分 $\int \frac{1}{x}\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x-1}}dx$。

## 答案与解析
**解**：令 $t = \sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x-1}}$，则 $x = \frac{t^2+1}{t^2-1}$，$dx = \frac{-4tdt}{(t^2-1)^2}$，于是：
$$\begin{aligned}
\int \frac{1}{x}\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x-1}}dx &= -4\int \frac{t^2 dt}{(t^2+1)(t^2-1)} = \int \left(\frac{1}{t+1} - \frac{1}{t-1} - \frac{2}{t^2+1}\right)dt \\
&= \ln\left|\frac{t+1}{t-1}\right| - 2\arctan t + C \\
&= \ln\left|\frac{\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x-1}}+1}{\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x-1}}-1}\right| - 2\arctan\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x-1}} + C
\end{aligned}$$
